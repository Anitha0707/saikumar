LIC Bima Lakshmi Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Bima Lakshmi (Plan 881) is a non-participating, non-linked plan designed exclusively for women. It combines life cover, periodic fixed money-back payments, limited illness cover and guaranteed additions.

Bima Lakshmi plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN881 / 512N389V01
Launched15 Oct 2025
Plan typeNon-par, non-linked, money-back plan for women
Entry age18 – 50 years
Policy term25 years
Premium paying term7 – 15 years
Minimum sum assured₹2,00,000 (multiples of ₹10,000), no maximum (underwriting)
Guaranteed additions7% of total tabular annual premium each year (in-force policies)
Money-back paymentsEvery 2 or 4 years, or after premiums cease (as chosen)

Key benefits of Bima Lakshmi

  • Life cover for the policyholder
  • Periodic fixed money-back payments
  • Limited illness cover included
  • Guaranteed maturity benefit and guaranteed additions
  • Optional Female Critical Illness Rider for extra premium
  • Auto cover facility after three full years' premiums are paid

Who is Bima Lakshmi best suited for?

Women who want protection with regular payouts and guaranteed benefits.
