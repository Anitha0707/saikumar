LIC Bima Jyoti Plan: Benefits & Eligibility

A non-linked, non-participating savings plan that adds a fixed amount to your sum assured every year during the premium paying term, so the maturity amount is known in advance.

Bima Jyoti plan at a glance

Plan typeNon-linked, non-participating savings plan
Policy term15 – 20 years
Guaranteed additions₹50 per ₹1,000 sum assured, every year in the premium paying term
Minimum sum assured₹1,00,000

Key benefits of Bima Jyoti

  • Guaranteed additions are added every year, giving certainty of returns
  • Death benefit includes the sum assured plus guaranteed additions
  • Maturity benefit includes the sum assured plus all guaranteed additions

Who is Bima Jyoti best suited for?

Conservative savers who prefer guaranteed, predictable benefits over market-linked returns.
