LIC Protection Plus Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Protection Plus (Plan 886) is a non-participating, unit-linked individual plan that combines market-linked savings with life cover. On maturity you receive the fund value plus a refund of the mortality charges deducted during the term.

Protection Plus plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN886 / 512L361V01
Launched3 Dec 2025
Plan typeNon-par, linked, individual savings plan with life cover
Entry age18 – 65 years
Policy term10, 15, 20 or 25 years
Premium paying term5, 7, 10 or 15 years
Minimum sum assured7 × annualised premium (age below 50) or 5 × (age 50 and above)
Maximum maturity ageUp to 90 years (25-year term)
ReturnsMarket-linked, not guaranteed

Key benefits of Protection Plus

  • Life cover throughout the policy term
  • Unit fund value paid at maturity
  • Refund of mortality charges deducted during the term, paid with the maturity benefit
  • Choice of fund type and option to pay top-up premiums
  • Option to increase or decrease sum assured, and partial withdrawals after 5 years (subject to conditions)

Who is Protection Plus best suited for?

Investors comfortable with market risk who want growth plus protection, with mortality charges returned at maturity.

Unit-linked returns depend on fund performance and are subject to market risk. Details are based on LIC's launch announcement. Rider options, charges, surrender terms and the full list of conditions are in the official sales brochure on licindia.in. Please confirm before buying.
