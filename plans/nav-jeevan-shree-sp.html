LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Single Premium Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Nav Jeevan Shree Single Premium (Plan 911) is a single-premium endowment plan with guaranteed additions. You pay one premium and the guaranteed additions accrue every year of the policy term.

Nav Jeevan Shree Single Premium plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN911 / 512N390V01
Launched4 Jul 2025
Plan typeSingle-premium endowment with guaranteed additions
Entry age30 days – 60 years (Option I), up to 40 years (Option II)
Age at maturity18 – 75 years (Option I), up to 60 years (Option II)
Policy term5 – 20 years
Minimum sum assured₹1,00,000, no maximum (underwriting)
Guaranteed additions₹85 per ₹1,000 of basic sum assured, every policy year

Key benefits of Nav Jeevan Shree Single Premium

  • One-time premium, no yearly payments to remember
  • Guaranteed additions accrue from inception to the end of the term
  • Life cover during the policy term

Who is Nav Jeevan Shree Single Premium best suited for?

People with a lump sum who want guaranteed, predictable growth.
