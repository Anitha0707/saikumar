LIC Jeevan Lakshya Plan: Benefits & Eligibility

A non-linked, with-profits endowment plan designed so that your family keeps receiving income if you are not around, and a lump sum is paid when the plan matures. Well suited to goals such as a child's education or marriage.

Jeevan Lakshya plan at a glance

Plan typeNon-linked, with-profits endowment
Entry age18 – 50 years
Policy term13 – 25 years
Premium paying termPolicy term minus 3 years
Minimum sum assured₹1,00,000
Annual income on death10% of basic sum assured, every year till maturity

Key benefits of Jeevan Lakshya

  • On death, your nominee receives 10% of the basic sum assured every year until the plan matures
  • The full sum assured plus accrued bonuses is paid at maturity, even after death
  • Future premiums are waived after death and the policy continues
  • On survival, the sum assured plus bonuses is paid at maturity
  • Tax benefits as per the prevailing Income Tax law

Who is Jeevan Lakshya best suited for?

Parents planning for a child's higher education or marriage who want protection and a guaranteed-style income for the family.
