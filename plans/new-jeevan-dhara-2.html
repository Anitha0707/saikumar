LIC New Jeevan Dhara-II Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's New Jeevan Dhara-II (Plan 892) is a non-participating, non-linked individual savings deferred annuity plan. You save during a deferment period and then receive a lifelong income at annuity rates guaranteed from the start.

New Jeevan Dhara-II plan at a glance

Plan no.892
Launched19 Oct 2026
Plan typeNon-par, non-linked, individual savings deferred annuity
Entry age18 – 65 or 70 years (depends on annuity option and deferment period)
Minimum vesting age30 years
Premium optionsSingle or regular premium
Annuity optionsSingle-life or joint-life

Key benefits of New Jeevan Dhara-II

  • Annuity rates are guaranteed from the start of the policy
  • Lifelong income after the deferment period
  • Life cover during the deferment period
  • Top-up annuity facility by paying additional premiums during deferment
  • Incentives for higher premiums, existing LIC policyholders and nominees of deceased policyholders

Who is New Jeevan Dhara-II best suited for?

People still earning who want to lock in a guaranteed retirement income today.
