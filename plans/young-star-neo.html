Young Star neo Health Insurance: Benefits & Eligibility

A health insurance option from Star Health designed for young adults and their families, offering a single private A/C room, automatic restoration, a cumulative bonus and wellness benefits.

Young Star neo plan at a glance

Entry age18 – 40 years (dependent children 91 days – 25 years)
Policy term1, 2 or 3 years
Policy typeIndividual or floater (up to 2 adults and 4 children)
Sum insured₹10L, ₹25L, ₹50L, ₹1 Cr or Unlimited
RoomSingle private A/C room
RestorationUp to 100%, unlimited times per year
Cumulative bonus50% per claim-free year, up to 100%
Health Booster100% of sum insured for every 7 claim-free years
Pre-hospitalisationUp to 90 days
Post-hospitalisationUp to 180 days
Air ambulanceUp to ₹5,00,000 per policy year

Key benefits of Young Star neo

  • Day-care, modern treatments and AYUSH covered up to the sum insured
  • Freeze Your Age: premium age stays at entry age until a claim is paid or age 55
  • Organ donor expenses and domiciliary hospitalisation covered
  • Unlimited teleconsultations and online mental-wellbeing consultations
  • Up to 3 gym or studio sessions per week at select locations (Stay Fit)
  • Premium waiver for a year on critical illness or accidental death

Who is Young Star neo best suited for?

Young professionals and newly married couples who want strong cover with wellness perks at a young-age premium.
