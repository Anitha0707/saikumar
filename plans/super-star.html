Super Star Health Insurance: Benefits & Eligibility

A comprehensive health insurance plan from Star Health with a wide choice of sum insured, unlimited automatic restoration, a no-claim bonus and many optional covers, for individuals and families.

Key benefits of Super Star

  • Cover for hospitalisation over 24 hours and all day-care procedures
  • Modern treatments and AYUSH covered up to the sum insured
  • Road ambulance and, if recommended, air ambulance
  • Premium waiver for the next year on critical illness or accidental death of the proposer
  • Newly wedded spouse, newborn or adopted child can be added mid-term
  • Unlimited teleconsultations on the Star Health app

Who is Super Star best suited for?

Individuals and families who want broad, high-limit health cover with strong restoration and bonus benefits.
