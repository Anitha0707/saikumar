LIC New Endowment Plan Plan: Benefits & Eligibility

A traditional non-linked, with-profits endowment plan that combines life cover with a disciplined savings habit. You receive the sum assured along with bonuses if you survive the term.

Key benefits of New Endowment Plan

  • Death benefit: sum assured plus accrued bonuses
  • Maturity benefit: sum assured plus bonuses and final additional bonus, where declared
  • Loan facility available once the policy acquires surrender value
  • Optional riders can add accident and other cover
  • Tax benefits as per the prevailing Income Tax law

Who is New Endowment Plan best suited for?

First-time buyers and families who want a simple, long-term savings plan with life cover.
