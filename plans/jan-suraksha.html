LIC Jan Suraksha Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Jan Suraksha (Plan 880) is a non-participating, non-linked life micro-insurance savings plan aimed at lower-income families. It gives life cover, guaranteed additions every year and a lump sum at maturity.

Jan Suraksha plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN880 / 512N388V01
Launched15 Oct 2025
Plan typeNon-par, non-linked, individual savings (micro insurance)
Entry age18 – 55 years
Policy term12 – 20 years
Premium paying termPolicy term minus 5 years
Sum assured₹1,00,000 – ₹2,00,000 (multiples of ₹5,000)
Guaranteed additions4% of annualised premium at the end of each policy year

Key benefits of Jan Suraksha

  • Financial support for the family if the policyholder dies during the term
  • Lump sum on maturity if the policyholder survives
  • Guaranteed additions every policy year
  • No medical examination for standard healthy lives
  • Auto cover facility after three full years' premiums are paid

Who is Jan Suraksha best suited for?

Families with modest incomes who want affordable cover plus savings.
