LIC Health Shield Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Health Shield (Plan 907) is a non-participating, non-linked individual health insurance plan that pays fixed benefits. It covers the principal insured and eligible family members.

Health Shield plan at a glance

Plan no.907
Launched19 Oct 2026
Plan typeNon-par, non-linked individual health plan (fixed benefit)
Principal insured age18 – 65 years
Children can be added90 days – 20 years
Policy term / premium paying term15 years each
PremiumsGuaranteed throughout the policy term

Key benefits of Health Shield

  • Fixed daily hospital cash allowance
  • Lump-sum payouts for major surgeries and critical illnesses
  • Guaranteed premiums for the whole term
  • No-claim benefit that increases the health cover
  • Premium waiver for other insured members after the principal insured's death (subject to conditions)
  • Riders available for additional premium

Who is Health Shield best suited for?

Families who want fixed, predictable health payouts on top of regular hospitalisation cover.
