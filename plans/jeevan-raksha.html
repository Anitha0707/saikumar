LIC Jeevan Raksha Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Jeevan Raksha (Plan 894) is a non-participating, non-linked pure risk plan. It pays a guaranteed, fixed death benefit during the policy term, with no bonuses or profit sharing, and is meant for people who want simple, affordable cover.

Key benefits of Jeevan Raksha

  • Guaranteed, fixed death benefit throughout the policy term
  • Special premium rates for women
  • Rebate for high sum assured
  • Choice of single, regular or limited premium payment
  • Optional riders available to enhance cover

Who is Jeevan Raksha best suited for?

Young earners and families who want low-cost, no-frills life cover.
