LIC New Cancer Cover Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's New Cancer Cover (Plan 908) is a fixed-benefit plan that pays on diagnosis of specified early-stage and major-stage cancers, regardless of actual treatment costs.

New Cancer Cover plan at a glance

Plan no.908
Launched19 Oct 2026
Plan typeFixed-benefit cancer cover (regular premium)
Entry age18 – 65 years
Sum assured optionLevel or increasing
Early-stage cancer25% of the applicable sum assured
Major-stage cancer100% of the applicable sum assured
Income to nominee1% of sum assured every month for 10 years (after major-stage cancer, on death, subject to terms)

Key benefits of New Cancer Cover

  • Payout on diagnosis, whatever the treatment cost
  • Covers specified early-stage and major-stage cancers (full list in the brochure)
  • Monthly income for the nominee for 10 years in the cases covered
  • Premium waiver after an eligible cancer diagnosis

Who is New Cancer Cover best suited for?

Anyone wanting an extra financial cushion against the cost and income loss of a cancer diagnosis.
