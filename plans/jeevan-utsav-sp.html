LIC Jeevan Utsav Single Premium Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Jeevan Utsav Single Premium (Plan 883) is a non-participating, non-linked, single-premium whole-life savings plan with guaranteed additions and a choice between regular and flexi income.

Jeevan Utsav Single Premium plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN883 / 512N392V01
Launched6 Jan 2026 (available from 12 Jan 2026)
Plan typeNon-par, non-linked, single-premium whole life savings plan
Entry age30 days – 65 years
Minimum sum assured₹5,00,000, no maximum (underwriting)
Guaranteed additions₹40 per ₹1,000 of basic sum assured, every policy year during the guaranteed additions period
Regular income (Option I)10% of basic sum assured every year, starting after 7 to 17 years (your choice)
Flexi income (Option II)10% accumulated and withdrawn later, earning 5.5% interest a year, compounded yearly

Key benefits of Jeevan Utsav Single Premium

  • Death benefit: higher of basic sum assured or 1.25 × single premium, plus accrued guaranteed additions
  • Maturity benefit on the same basis, plus accrued guaranteed additions
  • Loan facility available for liquidity
  • High sum assured rebate
  • Optional riders: Accidental Death and Disability Benefit Rider and New Term Assurance Rider

Who is Jeevan Utsav Single Premium best suited for?

People with a lump sum who want a lifelong guaranteed income stream.
