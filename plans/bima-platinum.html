LIC Bima Platinum Plan: Benefits & Eligibility NEW

LIC's Bima Platinum (Plan 770) is a non-participating, non-linked, limited-premium plan that combines protection with savings. It adds guaranteed additions during the premium paying term, pays a booster benefit, and then gives a regular income every year during the payout period. Launched to mark LIC's 70th anniversary.

Bima Platinum plan at a glance

Plan no. / UIN770 / 512N397V01
Launched1 Sep 2026 (available from 7 Sep 2026)

Entry age30 days – 55 years (varies with premium paying term)

Booster benefit70% of basic sum assured, on survival to end of premium paying term + 5 years
Regular income10% of basic sum assured every year during the payout period

Key benefits of Bima Platinum

  • Guaranteed additions build up every year while you pay premiums
  • Booster benefit of 70% of the basic sum assured on survival, five years after premiums end
  • Regular income of 10% of the basic sum assured every year in the payout period (policy term minus premium paying term)
  • Rebate for existing LIC policyholders and for nominees or beneficiaries of deceased policyholders
  • Optional riders can be added to enhance cover

Who is Bima Platinum best suited for?

People who want to pay premiums for a limited period and then enjoy guaranteed additions, a booster payout and a steady yearly income.
