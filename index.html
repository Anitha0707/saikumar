Which LIC plan is best for my child's education or marriage?

Popular LIC choices for children include Jeevan Tarun, Amritbaal, New Children's Money Back and Jeevan Lakshya. The right plan depends on the child's age, your budget and the year you need the money, so ask for a free comparison.

Which LIC plans give pure life cover (term insurance)?

LIC's term and protection plans on this site are Jeevan Amar, Tech Term, Yuva Term and Micro Bima. Term plans give high life cover at a low premium, mainly for family income protection.

Which LIC plans are suitable for pension and retirement?

LIC pension and retirement options include Jeevan Akshay, Saral Pension, Smart Pension and Jeevan Shanti. The best fit depends on whether you want an immediate annuity or income that starts later.
